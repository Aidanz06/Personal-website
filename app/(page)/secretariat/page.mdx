# <SecretariatMark />ecretariat

secretariat is a to-do app for students. it pulls canvas, syllabi and
calendars into one list for the day. i came on in october as a product
consultant to look at the interface, the code and the ai costs. it's at
[getsecretariat.com](https://www.getsecretariat.com).

<Slideshow project="secretariat" />

## what i worked on

the app did a lot already, but it didn’t feel like one product yet. the
buttons and dialogs had drifted into dozens of slightly different versions, so
i pulled them into one set. i also merged home and to do into a single screen,
so opening the app shows you what’s due.

## the look

we mocked up three directions and ended up using two. by day, the top of the
screen is a window and the sun sets as you check things off. at night, it’s a
lamp instead. the title just tells you how many are left.
