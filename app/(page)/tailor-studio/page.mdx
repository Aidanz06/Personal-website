# Tailor *Studio*

i resell clothes on grailed, and listing one piece used to take me 15 to 20
minutes. tailor studio is a desktop app i built to do most of it for me.

<Slideshow project="tailor-studio" />

## how it works

i drop in a folder of photos and it sorts them by piece, writes a draft
listing, and suggests a price from recent sales. if it's unsure about
something (usually the size) it asks me. then it fills out grailed's sell page
in chrome, and i check it over and hit post myself.

## testing it

i tried three ways of grouping photos on the same shoot. sending every photo
at once got all the pieces right for about a quarter of the cost. moving the
listing text to a smaller model made it 7x cheaper and the writing didn't get
worse. a listing costs about 7 cents now.

## where it's at

down to a few minutes an item. i use it every time i shoot, and so do a few
friends and family. no plans to sell it.
