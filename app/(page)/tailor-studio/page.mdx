# Tailor *Studio*

i sell clothes on grailed, and every listing used to take fifteen to twenty
minutes of the same work: sorting a shoot into items, writing a title and
description, checking what similar pieces sold for, then typing it all into
the form. tailor studio is a desktop app that does that part for me.

<Slideshow project="tailor-studio" />

## how it works

i point it at a folder of photos. it groups them by garment, reads the
brand, size and condition off the pictures, and drafts a listing with a
price from recent sold listings. anything it isn't sure about gets flagged
for me to check instead of guessed. one click types the draft into grailed's
sell form, and i look it over and press publish myself. it never signs in
and never submits anything.

## testing it

i tried three ways of grouping photos on the same test shoot. looking at
every photo together got all 9 items right and cost about a quarter as much
as going one photo at a time. moving the listing text to a smaller model
made it 7× cheaper with about the same quality. all in, it's around 7 cents
of ai per listing.

## where it's at

a few minutes an item now, instead of fifteen to twenty. i use it on every
shoot, and so do a few friends and family. it's a personal tool: no revenue,
and not built for any.
