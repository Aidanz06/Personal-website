# Tailor *Studio*

i resell clothes on grailed, and listing one item used to take me 15 to 20
minutes: sorting the photos, writing a title and description, looking up
what it's been selling for, and typing it all into the form. tailor studio
is a desktop app that does most of that for me.

<Slideshow project="tailor-studio" />

## how it works

i drop in a folder of photos. it figures out which photos go with which
piece, writes a draft listing, and suggests a price based on recent sales.
if it isn't sure about something, like a size, it asks me instead of
guessing. then it opens grailed's sell page in chrome and types everything
in. i look it over and post it myself. it never logs in or posts anything
on its own.

## testing it

i tried three ways of grouping photos on the same test shoot. looking at
all the photos at once got every piece right and cost about a quarter as
much. switching the listing text to a smaller model made it 7 times cheaper
with about the same quality. it comes out to around 7 cents of ai cost per
listing.

## where it's at

it takes a few minutes per item now. i use it every time i shoot, and a few
friends and family use it too. it's just a personal tool. i'm not selling
it.
