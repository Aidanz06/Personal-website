# tailor studio

i sell clothes on grailed, and every listing used to take fifteen to twenty
minutes of the same work: sorting a shoot into items, writing a title and
description, checking what similar pieces sold for, then typing it all into
the form. tailor studio is a desktop app that does that part for me.

<Slideshow />

## how it works

i point it at a folder of photos. it groups them by garment, reads the
brand, size, condition and colour off the pictures, and drafts a listing
with a price range from recent sold comparables. anything it isn't sure
about gets flagged for me to check rather than guessed. one click types the
draft into grailed's sell form, and i look it over and press publish myself.

it never signs in and never submits anything, so the account stays mine. and
it never calls something authentic, only a probable brand.

## where it's at

a few minutes an item now, instead of fifteen to twenty. i use it on every
shoot, and so do a few friends and family. it's a personal tool: no revenue,
and not built for any.
