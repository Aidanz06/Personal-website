# tailor studio

a desktop app that turns a folder of clothing photos into finished grailed
listings: grouped, written, priced against sold comparables, typed into the
sell form. i review and publish every one.

<Slideshow />

## problem

fifteen to twenty minutes an item, all of it manual:

- sorting a batch shoot into per-item groups
- writing a title and description from scratch
- pricing against recent sold listings
- filling the form, field by field

## approach

five stages, two of them mine:

- **import** — groups the shoot by garment; reads brand, category, size,
  condition and colour off the photos
- **draft** — title, description, and a price range from sold comparables,
  with a confidence rating
- **review** — mine: fix uncertain groups, confirm the key fields
- **fill** — types the draft into the sell form in one click
- **publish** — mine: nothing is submitted without me

## decisions

- **a form-fill assistant, not an auto-poster** — it never signs in and never
  submits; operating the account stays with me
- **ambiguity is flagged, not guessed** — anything the vision step is unsure
  of goes to review, not into a listing
- **no authenticity claims** — brand is reported as probable, never confirmed
- **accuracy tests gate the ai steps**, and a model sweep picked the cheapest
  configuration that still cleared them

## result

- a few minutes an item instead of fifteen to twenty
- used on every shoot, and by a few close friends and family
- a personal tool: no revenue, and not built for any
