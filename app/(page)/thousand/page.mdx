# <em>$</em>1,000

a daily game i've been working on. you name stuff you use, and find out what
$100 in the company behind it would've turned into.

<Slideshow project="thousand" />

## how it works

it's dec 31, 2019. each round you get a simple question, like "name something
in your kitchen," and 25 seconds. whatever you name, you buy $100 of the
company behind it and sell it at the end of 2025. say cheerios and you've
bought general mills.

after each answer you see what your $100 became next to every other answer
that counted. the better your pick did compared to the rest, the more floors
you get. the last round flips it: the game names a company and you guess what
$100 of it turned into.

after seven rounds your building stands next to real ones, from the flatiron
to the burj khalifa.

## the numbers

public companies put a small chart in their annual report showing what $100 in
their stock turned into over five years. the game uses those, straight from
sec filings. this prototype still has rough placeholder numbers; the real ones
go in once i've checked each one. not affiliated with any company shown.

## how i made it

i built it with claude. claude writes the code. the idea, the rules, the
questions and the look are mine.

## where it's at

friends are playtesting it. their main notes were that the old questions were
too hard and people missed that you buy on one date and sell on another. v5
fixes the questions. next is real numbers for every answer and a cleaner play
screen on laptop, then phone, sound and sharing.
