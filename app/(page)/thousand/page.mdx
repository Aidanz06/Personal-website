# <em>$</em>1,000

a daily game i've been making. you get a date from the past and six things
you could have bought then, like zoom stock, a pound of flour or a gold fund.
you put $500, $300 and $200 on three of them, then see what they turned into.

<Slideshow project="thousand" />

## how it works

pick a shelf, tap an item and give it one of the three amounts. move them
around until you're happy, then lock in. the reveal shows what $100 in each
became and how your three did. you can tap any row to see both prices, their
dates and where they came from.

## where the prices come from

only sec filings and us government data (bls, eia, usps, the federal
reserve), since those are free to republish. no estimates. it keeps the
shelves narrower than i'd like, so finding more things that fit is the main
thing i'm working on.

## where it's at

six shelves, in a beta with friends. next up are rooms so friends can see
each other's picks, more shelves, and a real name. "$1,000" is a working
title.
